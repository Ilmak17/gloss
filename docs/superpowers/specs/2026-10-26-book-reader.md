# Book reading mode

Approved in chat: an Apple Books-like single-page EPUB reader; left/right page turns, chapter navigation, bookmarks, full-book search, appearance controls, saved reading position, synchronized narration with automatic page turns, and Discuss without reflowing the book. English UI; Michael remains selected. Articles keep vertical scrolling.

Books and Articles have separate library tabs and entry points. Books accept EPUB/PDF; Articles accept URLs or pasted text. Existing documents, annotations and audio must survive. EPUB is the paginated format in this iteration; PDF keeps its existing extracted-text view, clearly labeled, without pretending to reproduce the original PDF layout.

Mount only the current EPUB chapter. Keep source block IDs and UTF-16 offsets for annotations and progress. Page numbers are chapter-local and change with typography; overall progress is text-based. Opening discussion overlays the page without changing its dimensions. Manual page turns suspend automatic following until Jump to audio is selected. No automatic paid prefetch.
